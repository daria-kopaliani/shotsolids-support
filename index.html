Espresso calculator support

Enter your dry coffee dose and espresso beverage mass in grams. Enter the percentage of total dissolved solids from your coffee refractometer.

Check a reading

An 18 g dose, 36 g beverage and 10% TDS give 20% extraction. The cup contains 3.6 g of dissolved coffee. Lower the reading to 9.5% and the yield changes to 19%.

Save and compare shots

Calculations are free. Shot Notebook is a one-time purchase. Tap Save shot to record a coffee, grinder setting and tasting notes. Save two shots and select Compare in Notebook. Open a shot to mark a favorite.

Restore a purchase

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open Method, select Purchase or restore, then Restore purchases. Recipes are stored on the device. Restoring the purchase restores access to the notebook.
