Shot Solids

Privacy policy

Shot Solids stores measurements, coffee names, grinder settings, and notes on your iPhone. Daria Kopaliani does not collect personal data through the app. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Purchases and links

Apple handles purchases through the App Store. Shot Solids reads the purchase status to unlock the notebook. Opening the method reference or a support link opens an external site with its own privacy practices.

Your records

Use Delete shot in a saved recipe to remove it. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Device backups follow your Apple settings.
